Career Background

Professional Experience

3+ years of experience in construction safety and workplace hazard prevention.

Construction Safety Officer

Construction Industry | 3 Years Experience

Responsible for supporting the implementation of site safety procedures, identifying hazards, monitoring work activities and promoting safe working practices among the workforce.

  • Conduct site safety inspections and identify unsafe acts and unsafe conditions.
  • Assist in preparing and reviewing HIRA, JSA, WMS and SWMS documentation.
  • Monitor compliance with permit-to-work requirements and approved work procedures.
  • Promote safe working practices for work at height, lifting activities and other high-risk construction tasks.
  • Conduct toolbox talks and communicate relevant safety precautions to workers.
  • Monitor the proper use of personal protective equipment (PPE).
  • Report unsafe conditions, near misses and incidents through the applicable site reporting process.
  • Follow up on corrective actions and support continuous improvement in workplace safety.
  • Conduct regular fire safety audits and ensure availability of firefighting equipment.
  • Manage emergency evacuation drills and maintain assembly point readiness.
  • Oversee confined space entry permits and gas testing procedures.
  • Audit scaffolding and temporary structures for structural integrity before use.
  • Maintain statutory safety records and ensure compliance with local labor laws.
  • Review subcontractor safety plans HIRA, work method statements, safe work method statements and legal-documents.
  • Organize site safety inductions for all new workers and visitors.
  • Conduct regular inspections of lifting gears and heavy machinery.
  • Preparing weekly and monthly reports to track safety performance.
  • Participating in internal safety audits to ensure regulatory compliance.
